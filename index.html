What I work on

Making optical brain monitoring quantitative enough to trust and small enough to wear: detectors with the dynamic range for dense arrays, compact control electronics, and calibration you can check.

  • ≈ 6 decadesdetector dynamic range with resistor-based SiPM gain switching
  • 76 dBpeak SNR, with about four decades quasi-linear (R² ≥ 0.9993)
  • 3.2 µVdark-noise floor on a 4 × 4 cm, 8 × 8 control module
  • 29 SiPMsbenchmarked by NEP, dynamic range and detectivity
